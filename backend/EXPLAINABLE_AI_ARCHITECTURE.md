**Version**: `v1.0.0`  
**System Module**: Module 6: Explainable AI (XAI)  
**Status**: Production-Ready / Operational  

---

## Executive Summary
The **Explainable AI (XAI) System** eliminates black-box AI outputs across RM Monitor. It provides human-understandable, audit-traceable, and quantitative explanations for every AI prediction, risk score, and recommendation directive generated by Modules 1 through 5.

The explanation engine (`xaiEngineService.js`) is strictly **decoupled** from core model execution. It computes normalized feature influence weights ($\sum w_i = 1.0$), traces telemetry data lineage, builds step-by-step natural language reasoning, and packages explanations into 4 reusable consumer adapters: **Dashboard**, **Digital Twin**, **Reports**, and **Manager Portal**.

## Mandatory 6-Attribute Explanation Payload Schema

Every explanation produced by the system strictly includes all 6 required fields:

| Field Name | Type | Description | Example |
|---|---|---|---|
| `prediction` | `string` | Clear output prediction statement | `"WARNING: PINK stock has breached safety limit threshold."` |
| `confidence_score` | `number` | Statistical confidence percentage ($0-100\%$) | `94` |
| `key_factors` | `Array<Factor>` | Weighted feature influence factors ($\sum w_i = 1.0$) | `[{ factor_name: "Stock Ratio", weight: 0.45, impact: "NEGATIVE" }]` |
| `reasoning` | `string[]` | Step-by-step natural language explanation | `["1. Evaluated materials balance...", "2. Analyzed withdrawal rate..."]` |
| `suggested_action` | `string` | Direct operational directive | `"Reorder safety batch of 75.0 KG of PINK."` |
| `data_sources_used`| `string[]` | Telemetry audit lineage array | `["materials", "transactions", "alerts", "qr_history"]` |

## Reusable Consumer Portal Adapters

1. **Dashboard Adapter (`getDashboardExplanations()`)**:
   - URL: `/api/ai/xai/dashboard`
   - Purpose: Provides executive-level transparency cards summarizing overall inventory health and rack capacity explanations.

2. **Digital Twin Adapter (`getDigitalTwinExplanations()`)**:
   - URL: `/api/ai/xai/digital-twin`
   - Purpose: Maps node-level XAI explanations directly to physical 3D/2D rack coordinates and material assets.

3. **Reports Adapter (`getReportsExplanations()`)**:
   - URL: `/api/ai/xai/reports`
   - Purpose: Generates audit-ready tabular report matrices suitable for export (PDF/Excel) with complete feature weight attributions.

4. **Manager Portal Adapter (`getManagerPortalExplanations()`)**:
   - URL: `/api/ai/xai/manager-portal`
   - Purpose: Filters high-priority and critical operational directives for decision audit trails and manager approval workflows.

## Feature Influence Attribution & Lineage Tracing

- **Feature Influence Weighting**: Each contributing factor is assigned a normalized weight $w_i \in [0.0, 1.0]$ such that $\sum_{i=1}^n w_i = 1.0$. The impact direction is classified as `POSITIVE` (improves health), `NEGATIVE` (increases risk), or `NEUTRAL`.
- **Data Lineage Audit**: Traces exact database table origins (`materials`, `transactions`, `material_usage_history`, `racks`, `rack_inventory`, `qr_history`, `alerts`, `audit_logs`).
